Welcome to MoiConnect – the ultimate all-in-one mobile app designed specifically for Moi University students and campus community! 

Whether you are looking for academic resources, revision materials, student housing, or staying updated with campus events, MoiConnect brings everything you need directly to your fingertips.

---

KEY FEATURES:

🎓 1. ACADEMIC HUB & REVISION MATERIALS
• Past Papers & CAT Papers: Access a comprehensive repository of past examination papers and Continuous Assessment Tests across all schools, faculties, and courses.
• Lecture Notes & Study Guides: Download and view organized course notes, revision guides, and module summaries on the go.
• Built-in Smart PDF Viewer: View documents smoothly within the app with page navigation, zoom, and instant download capabilities.
• Smart Download Counters: Easily track popular and high-yield revision materials with real-time download metrics.

🏠 2. LANDLORD PORTAL & OFF-CAMPUS HOUSING
• Hostel & Apartment Listings: Browse verified off-campus rentals near Main Campus, Annex, Town Campus, and surrounding areas.
• Room Features & Amenities: View detailed property descriptions, pricing, deposit requirements, distance to campus, and included amenities (water, Wi-Fi, security).
• Direct Landlord Contact: Connect directly with property owners and managers without broker hassle.

📰 3. CAMPUS NEWS, EVENTS & ANNOUNCEMENTS
• Official & Student Updates: Stay informed with real-time updates regarding semester schedules, exam timetables, fee deadlines, and campus news.
• Trending Events: Never miss out on campus events, career fairs, sports tournaments, and student organization activities.

💬 4. COMMUNITY & STUDENT SERVICES
• Student Marketplace: Buy and sell used textbooks, revision packs, electronic gadgets, and household items safely within the student community.
• Student Profile & Customization: Keep track of your academic details, course year, school, and saved study materials.
• Instant Offline Access: Access previously downloaded papers and study notes even when offline or low on data!
