Account & Data Privacy

How do I request deletion of my account and associated data?

MoiConnect provides two methods to request account and personal data deletion:

  • In-App Deletion (Instant): Open the Profile tab in the app -> scroll to the bottom -> tap "Delete Account" -> confirm. Your profile and login data are immediately purged.
  • Email Request: Send an email to amanikbt1@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" using your registered email address. Include your full name and registered phone number. Requests are processed within 5 business days.
Account & Data Privacy

What user data is deleted or retained upon request?

Upon receiving your deletion request, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Personal Profile: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Password hashes.
  • User Content: Avatar photos, saved offline bookmarks, and private landlord message history.
  • Landlord Records: Verification applications and submitted identity details.

Note: Publicly uploaded academic study materials remain on the platform for community access, but will be completely anonymized to disassociate your identity.

Academic Resources

How do I access and download past papers or notes?

Navigate to the Academics tab or click Quick Access cards on the Home Screen. Search by course code (e.g., COM 310, STA 210) to read materials in the PDF viewer or tap Download for offline access.

Downloads & Storage

Where are my downloaded materials saved?

All downloaded past papers and summaries appear in the Downloads tab. You can manage files or tap the 3-dots menu to clear device storage at any time.
