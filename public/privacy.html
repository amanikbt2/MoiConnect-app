Welcome to MoiConnect ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected through our mobile app and platform services. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and user rights.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver student services, academic resource sharing, and rental house booking connections:

3. How We Use Information

Your information is processed strictly for the following purposes:

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, trade, or monetize your personal data. Data is shared exclusively with landlords upon your explicit request when making a rental inquiry, or to satisfy legal obligations.
