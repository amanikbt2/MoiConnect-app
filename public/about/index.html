A digital student companion for Kenya

University notes, past papers, CATs and student collaboration. All in one place.

MConnect brings academic resources, student rentals and campus communities together in one easy-to-use app for Kenyan university students, including students at Moi University. The MConnect Android app is available to download from Google Play.

Download MConnect on Google Playhttps://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amanikbt1.moiconnectOpen the MConnect web app

What you can do

Study and campus life, together

Find materials for your courses, keep useful resources close, and connect with other students through MConnect.

📚

Find academic resources

Browse past examination papers, revision notes, lecture slides and CAT preparation materials across university faculties.

🔎

Search by unit code

Look for study materials by unit code, course name or department, then preview PDFs in the app.

📥

Study offline

Download learning resources for offline reading when you’re away from an internet connection.

🏠

Explore student rentals

Browse off-campus housing listings and compare details such as rent, deposits, location and amenities.

💬

Connect with students

Join campus discussion spaces and share revision notes, past papers and study guides with fellow students.

🔊

Listen to study notes

Use text-to-speech to listen to supported notes while studying or on the move.

The person behind MConnect

Developed by Noman Keli

Noman Keli is the developer of MConnect, a computer science student at Moi University and a cybersecurity enthusiast. He created MConnect to bring useful study resources and campus connections together for university students. He is planning to establish Keysire Inc., a technology company.
